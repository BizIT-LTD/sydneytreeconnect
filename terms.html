Referral terms

Last updated: 4 November 2026

These terms explain what Sydney Tree Connect does, and doesn’t do, when you send an enquiry through sydneytreeconnect.com.

What Sydney Tree Connect is

Sydney Tree Connect is an enquiry and referral website run by an individual. Sydney Tree Connect is the name of the website, not a company. We receive enquiries from Sydney property owners and others, review them and forward them by email to a tree contractor.

We do not carry out tree work, assess trees, give quotes or decide whether approvals are needed. We are not a comparison service.

What happens after you submit an enquiry

  1. We receive your enquiry by email and review it.
  2. We forward your details and any photos to the contractor, as you agreed when you ticked the consent box.
  3. The contractor reviews your enquiry and may contact you using your preferred contact method to discuss the job, check required permissions and provide a quote.
  4. If you accept, you arrange the work, timing and payment directly with the contractor.

We forward enquiries manually. We can’t promise that the contractor will contact you within a particular time, take on your job or offer a particular price.

The contractor’s responsibilities

Your agreement for any tree work is with the contractor, not with Sydney Tree Connect. The contractor is responsible for:

  • the assessment and quote, including whether they charge for an assessment or quote
  • checking your authority, the tree’s identity and any approval documents and conditions before work starts
  • carrying out the work safely and in line with any approval conditions
  • their own pricing, payment terms, licences, insurance and guarantees.

Before you agree to any work, ask the contractor for their written quote and terms, and check their insurance and any qualifications that matter to you.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate details, and only submit an enquiry about property you own or are authorised to act for. If you are unsure, you can still ask for guidance, but your enquiry does not authorise any work.
  • Only attach photos you are entitled to share, and avoid photos of people.
  • Obtain any council permit, development consent or other approval that applies, or documented evidence of an exemption. The general guidance on this website is not legal advice and is not a decision about your tree.

Not an emergency service

The enquiry form is not monitored for emergencies. If a tree has fallen on a building or power lines, or anyone is in danger, call Triple Zero (000). For storm and flood help, contact the NSW SES on 132 500.

Fees

There is no charge to submit an enquiry through this website. Sydney Tree Connect does not take payments from customers.

Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other laws that can’t be excluded.

Privacy

Our privacy notice explains how we handle your information, including sharing it with the contractor.

Contact and changes

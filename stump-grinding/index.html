Stump grinding enquiries in Sydney

If a tree has already been removed and the stump is still there, send one enquiry about grinding it down. We forward your enquiry to a tree contractor for review. They may contact you to discuss the job and provide a quote. Availability and a response are not guaranteed.

Start a stump grinding enquiry How it works

Before you enquire

  • Has the tree been removed yet? If not, choose “Tree removal” in the form and mention in the job description that you would also like the stump ground.
  • Check any permit conditions. If the tree was removed under a council permit, the permit may include conditions such as replacement planting. Hornsby and Northern Beaches councils both describe replanting requirements for approved removals.
  • Think about underground services. Pipes and cables can run near stumps. Before You Dig Australia provides information about underground services, and the contractor can advise on what checks they make.

What to include in your enquiry

  • How many stumps there are and roughly how wide each one is.
  • Where they are, and what is nearby: paths, fences, garden beds or the house.
  • Access: the narrowest gate or path a machine would need to pass through, and any steps or slopes.
  • Whether there are large surface roots, and what you plan to do with the area afterwards.

Photos that help

Photos are optional. Useful photos show each stump with something for scale, such as a shoe or a tape measure, the area around it, and the access path from the street. You can attach up to 5 photos, each up to 4 MB and 12 MB in total. Large photos are resized in your browser before sending. Please don’t include photos of people.

What happens after you send your enquiry

  1. We receive your enquiry by email and review it. This is done manually, so it isn’t instant.
  2. We forward your details and any photos to a tree contractor for review, as you agree to when you tick the consent box.
  3. They may contact you to discuss the job by your preferred method: email, or phone if you choose it and include your number.
  4. If they can take on the job, they assess it, explain what approvals may apply and provide a quote.
  5. You decide whether to go ahead. Any work, timing and payment are arranged directly with the contractor.

Sending an enquiry is free. It is not a booking, a contract or approval to remove or prune a tree. Sydney Tree Connect does not carry out tree work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enquire about stump grinding on its own?

Yes. Choose “Stump grinding” in the form. If the tree is still standing, choose “Tree removal” instead and mention in the job description that you would also like the stump ground.

Do I need council approval to grind a stump?

We haven’t found council guidance that deals specifically with grinding the stump of a tree that has already been lawfully removed, so we don’t say either way. If the tree was removed under a permit, check its conditions, such as replacement planting, and ask your council if you’re unsure.

Will the roots and grindings be removed too?

Ask the contractor. Quotes can differ in how deep the stump is ground, whether surface roots are included and whether the grindings are taken away, so check what the written quote covers.

Ready to ask about stump grinding?

Tell us how many stumps there are and how to reach them. The contractor may contact you to discuss the job and provide a quote.

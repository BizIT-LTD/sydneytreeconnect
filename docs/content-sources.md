## Statements deliberately not made

- No statement that ownership, a dead tree, perceived danger or a tree's size automatically permits removal.
- No universal Sydney exemption, and no specific size thresholds (these vary by council DCP).
- No council-specific rules. There are more than 30 councils in Greater Sydney with different DCPs, so we link to the OLG council finder rather than summarising each one. If council-specific pages are added later, verify each statement against that council's current DCP and record it here.
- No DIY felling advice.

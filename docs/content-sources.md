## Statements deliberately not made

- No statement that ownership, a dead tree, perceived danger or a tree's size automatically permits removal.
- No universal Sydney exemption, and no specific size thresholds (these vary by council DCP).
- Council-specific rules only for Ku-ring-gai, Hornsby and Northern Beaches, each attributed to that council's own page (“Council says…”). Other councils are covered by the OLG council finder. Exemptions that the councils still list for dead or hazardous trees are reported with a warning to confirm in writing, because the NSW policy changes page (updated 23 Oct 2026) says previous dead and dying tree exemptions no longer apply.
- No council DCP PDFs are linked directly. Their content couldn't be verified on 4 Nov 2026, so the guides link to the council pages that link to them.
- No tree removal price ranges. On 4 Nov 2026 the published Sydney ranges found came only from commercial and lead-generation websites and disagreed widely. The cost guide explains the cost factors and quotes only official council fees, with date and source.
- The council guides say they don't indicate contractor coverage.
- No DIY felling advice.

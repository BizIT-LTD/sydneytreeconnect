Privacy notice

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This notice explains how Sydney Tree Connect handles the personal information you send us through the enquiry form on sydneytreeconnect.com. We’ve tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are

Sydney Tree Connect is an enquiry and referral website. It is run by an individual. Sydney Tree Connect is the name of the website, not a company. We receive tree service enquiries and pass them to a tree contractor. We do not carry out tree work.

Privacy contact: sydneytree278@gmail.com

What we collect

  • The details you enter in the form: your name, email address, phone number (if you provide one), preferred contact method, suburb and postcode, the service you need, your job description, your connection to the property and your council approval status.
  • Any photos you attach. Large photos are resized in your browser before sending, which also removes most embedded information such as location data. Photos sent in other formats may still contain that information.
  • A record of your consent: the wording you agreed to, and the date and time you submitted the form.
  • Technical details sent with the form: your IP address and browser type. We use these to keep a consent record and to prevent spam and misuse.

We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies, and we don’t track you across other websites.

Why we collect it

  • To pass your enquiry to a tree contractor so they can contact you, assess the job and provide a quote.
  • To answer questions about your enquiry.
  • To keep a record that you agreed to your details being shared.
  • To protect the form from spam and misuse.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it to send you marketing.

Who we share it with

  • The tree contractor. When you tick the consent box, we forward your enquiry details and photos to the tree contractor we pass enquiries to. Once the contractor has your details, they handle them under their own privacy practices. You can ask them directly how they store and use your information.
  • Our service providers. The form is processed by our website host, Hostinger, and sent through and received in our enquiry inbox, which is a Gmail account provided by Google. They store and process information on our behalf.
  • Where required by law. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Overseas storage: Hostinger and Google may store or process this information on servers outside Australia.

How we store it and how long we keep it

The website does not keep a database of enquiries. Your enquiry, including photos, is sent to our enquiry inbox by email and is not saved on the website. Uploaded photos exist on the web server only while the form is being processed. To limit spam, the server keeps a short-lived record of submission times linked to a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address. It is automatically deleted after about two days.

Enquiry emails, including forwarded copies, stay in our email account until we delete them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time (see below). Our email account is protected by a password and two-factor authentication.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing sydneytree278@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. If we have already passed your enquiry to the contractor, we will tell you. Deleting our copy does not delete the contractor’s copy, so you can also ask the contractor directly.

Your choices

Photos and a phone number are optional. If you’d rather not have your details shared with a contractor, please don’t submit the form. Without that consent we can’t pass your enquiry on.

Questions and complaints

If you have a concern about how we handled your information, contact us first at sydneytree278@gmail.com. You can also find information about privacy complaints on the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner website.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if the way we handle enquiries changes, and change the “last updated” date above.
